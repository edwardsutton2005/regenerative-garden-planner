Privacy

Last updated October 17, 2026

Without an account

Your garden is saved only in your own browser. Nothing about it is sent to this site, and clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

With an account

When you sign in, the planner stores:

  • your email address;
  • if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture link Google shares with sign-in;
  • your gardens: their names, sizes, beds, plants, and sunlight.

This is kept with Supabase, which provides sign-in and the database. Each person's gardens can be read and changed only by that person's account.

What isn't done with it

Your data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. The site has no analytics or tracking cookies. Your browser's storage holds only what the planner needs: your browser garden, your sign-in session, and settings like whether the gardener is shown.

The site is hosted by Vercel, and its font comes from Google Fonts. Like any website, they receive your IP address when your browser loads a page.

Deleting your data

You can delete any of your gardens yourself, from My gardens in the planner's menu. To delete your account and everything stored with it, or to ask what's stored, email edwardsutton2005@gmail.com.
